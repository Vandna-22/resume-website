16S rRNA Metagenomics Analysis in OPMD & OSCC: From QIIME2 to Machine Learning

In our recent work, we systematically extracted papers related to case-control studies in OPMD (Oral Potentially Malignant Disorders) and OSCC (Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma) patients. We specifically focused on studies utilizing DNA Extraction followed by 16S rRNA sequence analysis.

Our comprehensive bioinformatics workflow leveraged QIIME2 for end-to-end processing. The pipeline included:

  • Denoising the sequence data to ensure high-quality representative sequences.
  • Generating detailed Feature Tables spanning taxonomic assignments from Level 2 (Phylum) down to Level 7 (Species).
  • Computing and visualizing Alpha and Beta Diversity via clear bar plots and interactive QZV files.

Following the core taxonomic profiling, we successfully applied Machine Learning models on the microbiome feature tables to identify predictive microbial signatures across patient cohorts.

Acknowledgments: I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Dr. Pramod Kumar (Scientist-D) and Dr. Akanksha (Project Scientist) for their invaluable guidance and assistance. A very special thanks to Mr. Manupal Chaudhary (Technical Officer-B) for his exceptional and unwavering technical support, which made the execution of this complex pipeline seamless.
