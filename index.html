About Me

I am Vandna Gupta, an M.Sc. Bioinformatics student at Chaudhary Charan Singh University with active research experience at NICPR Noida.


My work focuses on metatranscriptomics pipeline development, microbiome profiling, NGS analysis, KEGG pathway interpretation, and AI-integrated computational biology workflows.


I work with tools including Kraken2, Bowtie2, FastQC, Trimmomatic, DESeq2, edgeR, RStudio, Python, Linux, and AI tools like Claude AI, Gemini CLI, AntiGravity AI, and OpenCode AI.
